# ShopEasy Shipping and Delivery

## Options
- Standard: 3-5 business days, free for orders over $50, otherwise $4.99.
- Express: 1-2 business days, $14.99.
- International: 7-14 business days, calculated at checkout. Customs fees are paid by the customer.

## Tracking and delays
- A tracking number is emailed when the order ships.
- If a package is more than 7 business days late, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation.

## Changing the address
- The shipping address can be changed only while the order status is "processing".
- After shipment, the address cannot be changed; contact the carrier.
