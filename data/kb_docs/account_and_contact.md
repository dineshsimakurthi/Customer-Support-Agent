# ShopEasy Account and Contact

## Account help
- Reset a password with "Forgot password" on the login page; the reset link expires after 30 minutes.
- Email address can be changed under Account > Profile after verifying the new address.
- Newsletter: unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of any email, or under Account > Preferences.

## Contact
- Live chat and email: 24/7. Phone: Mon-Fri 9am-6pm.
- Customers can ask for a human agent at any time.
