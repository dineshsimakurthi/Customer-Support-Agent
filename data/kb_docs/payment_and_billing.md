# ShopEasy Payment and Billing

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash or cheques.

## Common problems
- Double charge: a second "pending" authorization usually drops off within 3-5 business days. If both charges are completed, we refund the duplicate within 5 business days.
- Declined card: check the card details and billing address, or try another method.
- Money taken but order not created: contact support with the transaction date and amount.

## Invoices
- A PDF invoice is emailed after every order and is available under Account > Orders > Invoice.
- Company invoices with a VAT/Tax ID can be requested within 30 days of purchase.
