# ShopEasy Refund Policy

## Eligibility
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.

## Timeline
- Refunds are issued 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- Customers can track refund status under Account > Orders > Refund status.

## Special cases
- Damaged or wrong item: full refund plus free return shipping.
- Returns after 30 days: store credit only.
- **Refunds over $500 require manager approval.** Support agents must not promise these refunds; escalate to a human.
