# ShopEasy Cancellation Policy

- Orders can be cancelled free of charge while the status is "processing".
- Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; the customer can refuse delivery or return it within 30 days under the Refund Policy.
- Cancelling a shipped order or an order with custom engraving may incur a 10% restocking fee.
- Cancelled orders are refunded within 3-5 business days to the original payment method.
